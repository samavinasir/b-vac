B-vac: A Robust Software Package For Bacterial Vaccine Design

A standalone, offline reverse vaccinology pipeline that screens bacterial proteomes to identify potential vaccine candidates — combining localization, non-host homology, virulence factor, and epitope mapping analysis in one user-friendly interface, with no internet connection or coding required.

Download Now
1

Localization

Flags surface-exposed and secreted proteins accessible to the host immune system.

2

Non-Host Homologs

Filters out proteins similar to host sequences to reduce autoimmunity risk.

3

Virulence Factors

Matches candidates against a curated database of known bacterial virulence factors.

4

Epitope Mapping

Predicts B-cell and T-cell (CD4+/CD8+) epitopes for immunogenicity assessment.
